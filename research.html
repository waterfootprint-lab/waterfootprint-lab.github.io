Research

At the global scale, the lab assesses water scarcity and the water footprints of crops, livestock, and international trade. Across the United States, it addresses drought and low-flow risk, streamflow prediction, livestock water use, and PFAS releases from consumer products. At the basin scale, it examines agricultural water use under water rights in the Upper Colorado River Basin and the effects of groundwater and surface-water withdrawals on streamflow in the Mississippi Embayment. These studies combine process-based hydrologic and crop modeling and water footprint accounting with satellite remote sensing and machine learning.
