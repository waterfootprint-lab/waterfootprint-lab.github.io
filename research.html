Research

Research themes of the Water Security Lab

The Water Security Lab studies how human activities, environmental change, and hydrologic processes interact to shape water availability, use, quality, and risk. Our research spans five interconnected areas.

Research Areas
01

Water Footprints & Sustainable Water Use

Quantifying water use and its drivers across agricultural, food, livestock, and economic systems to identify opportunities for more sustainable water management.

02

Water Scarcity, Drought & Low-Flow Risk

Assessing water availability, drought, low-flow conditions, and associated risks to people, ecosystems, and critical sectors.

03

Groundwater & Surface-Water Systems

Understanding groundwater use, groundwater-surface-water interactions, and their implications for hydrologic systems and water security.

04

Water-Energy-Food Systems

Examining interactions and trade-offs among water, energy, food production, and emerging water demands.

05

PFAS Footprints & Water-Quality Risk

Assessing PFAS footprints, pathways, and water-related risks across production and supply systems.

Our Approaches

We combine hydrologic modeling, remote sensing, geospatial analysis, and data science to address these interconnected water-security challenges across local to global scales.
